Frequently asked questions

Does Symfino One use a camera?

No. It uses 60GHz mmWave radar and does not capture images.

Does anyone need to wear anything?

No wearable is required.

How do alerts work?

Fall detection push notifications are sent through the Tuya Smart app when 2.4GHz Wi-Fi and internet are available. Optional SMS and phone-call alerts may require additional fees.

Can alerts be missed or incorrect?

Yes. False or missed alerts are possible due to positioning, obstructions, room conditions, pets, multiple people, or slow and unusual falls.

Is Symfino One a medical device?

No. It is not a medical device or a substitute for emergency assistance.

Who is the monitoring designed for?

It is intended primarily for one person in the covered room.

Do I need a subscription to receive fall alerts?

No. Symfino One sends fall detection push notifications through the Tuya Smart app without a mandatory subscription. Optional SMS and phone-call alerts may incur additional charges.

What are the shipping and warranty details?

Estimated Shipping: Approximately 10 Business Days. This estimate is for shipping to the United States. It is not a guaranteed delivery date. Symfino One includes a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. If your device experiences a qualifying defect during the warranty period, we will provide a replacement at no additional cost. We do not accept returns for change of mind.

Our promise

Symfino One gives families a discreet way to stay informed while helping preserve independence, privacy, and dignity. It is not a medical device or a substitute for emergency assistance.
