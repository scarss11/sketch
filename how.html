How it works

Monitors the room

Place Symfino One on a shelf or the wall in the room you want covered. It senses movement with 60GHz radar. It does not use a camera, and nobody has to wear anything.

Identifies a potential fall

It looks for movement that may be a fall, such as a person going down and staying low. It is meant for one person in that room. A slow fall, a pet, or someone else in the room can change what it detects.

Sends a phone alert

When the home has 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, the alert can reach your phone through the Tuya or Smart Life app. It is not a medical device and it does not call emergency services. Alerts can be missed.
