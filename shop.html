How it works

  1. It monitors the room. Put Symfino One on a shelf or the wall. It senses movement with 60GHz radar. There is no camera and nothing to wear.
  2. It looks for a possible fall. If someone goes down and stays low, it can flag that movement. It is meant for one person in the room.
  3. It alerts your phone. With 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, a fall detection push notification can arrive in the Tuya Smart app. There is no mandatory subscription. Optional SMS and phone-call alerts may require additional fees. It does not call emergency services, and an alert can be missed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it use a camera?

No. Symfino One uses 60GHz mmWave radar. It does not record images or video.

Is there anything to wear?

No. Nothing to wear.

What Wi-Fi do I need?

2.4GHz Wi-Fi is required for remote alerts.

How are alerts sent?

Fall detection push notifications are sent through the Tuya Smart app when Wi-Fi and internet are available.

Do I need a subscription to receive fall alerts?

No. Symfino One sends fall detection push notifications through the Tuya Smart app without a mandatory subscription. Optional SMS and phone-call alerts may incur additional charges.

Is it a medical device?

No. It is not a medical device or a substitute for emergency assistance.

Alerts can be missed. Positioning, obstructions, room conditions, pets, multiple people, and slow or unusual falls can affect detection.
